# kinder.

A small awareness site against animal testing. It opens with "I believe in your cause." and then "And I believe you can end animal testing.", followed by a circle reveal into the hero photo.

**What's on the page**
- The numbers: 192.1M animals a year, 89% drug failure rate, 44 countries with cosmetics bans (all sourced)
- Flip cards for the animals most used in labs, with a fun fact on each
- Three games: *Free them* (open the cages), *Myth or fact* (swipe cards) and *Memory*
- The science replacing animal tests, plus a timeline of progress (2004 to 2025)
- Five actions to tick off and a downloadable pledge card

Everything lives in `index.html`, so there's no build step. Photos are hotlinked from Unsplash under the Unsplash License, and photographers are credited in the footer. Facts are cited in the footer sources list.
